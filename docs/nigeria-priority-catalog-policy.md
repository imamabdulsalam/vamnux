# Nigeria-Priority Catalog Policy

## Purpose

VAMNUX will keep its international marketplace structure but default its live Gaming Top-Up listings to a focused Nigeria-priority view. The view is a merchandising filter, not an assertion that a supplier has guaranteed eligibility in Nigeria. Each selected service remains a real, active FlashTopUp record with the supplier-required account fields and price shown on its detail page.

## Evidence used

Carry1st's Nigeria Direct Game Top-Up page currently presents PUBG Mobile, Mobile Legends, Free Fire, Arena Breakout, BIGO Live, and Ragnarok Origin among its Nigeria-facing direct top-up products. [1] Techpoint reports PUBG Mobile's local-server investment in Nigeria and notes CODM's Nigerian server launch; it also describes PUBG Mobile as the second most downloaded mobile game globally in 2024. [2]

These sources establish market relevance for the named game families; they do **not** establish that a particular FlashTopUp regional SKU can be used by every Nigerian player.

## Visibility rules

The default **Nigeria focus** view will prioritise only currently synchronised VAMNUX families that overlap the Nigeria-facing evidence or explicitly state a global scope: `PUBG Mobile`, `Mobile Legends Global`, `Free Fire Global`, `Arena Breakout`, `Bigo Live Diamonds`, and `Ragnarok Origin`.

Families explicitly named for another territory or market, such as `Free Fire Indonesia`, `Free Fire Bangladesh`, `Free Fire Europe`, `Free Fire Brazil`, `Free Fire LATAM`, `Free Fire Middle East`, `Free Fire SG`, `Free Fire SGMY`, `Free Fire Taiwan`, `Free Fire Vietnam`, regional Valorant variants, and `Mobile Legends Russia`, will be hidden from the default view. They remain in the database and are available through a clearly labelled **All supplier catalog** control for future international expansion.

The detail page will keep the supplier-region guidance visible and continue to require the player details supplied by FlashTopUp. VAMNUX will not state that a service is Nigeria-compatible unless the supplier provides a specific Nigeria eligibility signal.

## Verification

The public VAMNUX catalog defaults to the six real Nigeria-priority families. Its **All supplier catalog** control was verified to reveal the full synchronised supplier view while retaining the same compact listing and service-detail architecture. The international view is therefore an explicit expansion option rather than an erased catalog.
